# GOAL.md

## Primary Objective
Deliver a **1 kg** probe to an asymptotic solar-system escape speed of **0.001c** (≈300 km/s).

### Why 0.001c?
- ~63 AU/yr — reaches 1000 AU in ~16 years, 550 AU (near interstellar medium) in ~9 years.
- Order-of-magnitude leap beyond Voyager (~3.6 AU/yr) and current Interstellar Probe concepts (~7–8 AU/yr).
- Still non-relativistic, but transformative for precursor science.

## Success Criteria
1. Credible end-to-end trajectory and mass budget showing ≥300 km/s asymptotic for a 1 kg dry probe.
2. All major elements use technology at TRL ≥3 or have clear near-term development paths (chemical stages, solar thermal, tethers, Starship-class refueling).
3. Interactive prototype that lets users explore speed contributions from different methods.
4. Multi-agent review of the leading concepts with documented trade-offs.

## Non-Goals (for this phase)
- Full spacecraft detailed design (power, comms, instruments beyond 1 kg allocation).
- Relativistic propulsion or pure laser sails as the primary path (may be considered as alternatives).
- Human-rated systems.

## Key Constraints
- Chemical Isp ceiling ~450 s (LH2/LOX).
- Rocket equation makes pure chemical Δv of hundreds of km/s impossible without absurd mass ratios.
- Must leverage Oberth effect, external momentum sources (tethers, hitchhikes, planets), and infrastructure.

## Roadmap Phases
1. **Concept Catalog** — Document all boost methods and realistic ceilings.
2. **Integrated Architecture** — Combine best elements into 2–3 reference missions.
3. **Prototype Engine** — Web tool to visualize cumulative speed.
4. **Multi-Agent Critique** — Formal reviews and refinements.
5. **Mass & Trajectory Validation** — Spreadsheet or simple code models.
