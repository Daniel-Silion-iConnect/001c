# DESIGN.md — Concept of Operations

## High-Level Architecture
A staged sequence that borrows energy from multiple sources:

1. **Earth departure + orbital assembly** — Refueled chemical stages provide high C3.
2. **Inner solar system maneuvering** — Gravity assists (Venus/Earth/Mars) to set up solar dive or Jupiter encounter.
3. **Momentum-exchange tether chain** — Successive rotovators in elliptical orbits add cumulative Δv (tens of km/s).
4. **Deep Solar Oberth** — Burn or solar-thermal thrust at low perihelion multiplies energy.
5. **Optional hitchhike** — Tether attachment to a fast interstellar object or high-speed asteroid for final incremental boost.
6. **Cruise** — Asymptotic escape; minimal systems for science and comms.

## Reference Concepts (to be refined)
### Concept A: Chemical + Solar Oberth + Tether Chain
- Starship refueling → high C3 → Jupiter assist to solar dive → Oberth burn → tether chain outbound.
- Expected ceiling: 100–200 km/s for 1 kg class with aggressive assumptions.

### Concept B: Sundiver Hybrid (Sail + Chemical)
- Light solar sail + close perihelion + prograde burn.
- Better for ultra-light probes; 1 kg is borderline but possible with high area-to-mass.

### Concept C: Infrastructure-Heavy
- Pre-deployed chain of 10–20 momentum-exchange tethers + final nuclear-thermal or solar-thermal Oberth.
- Highest potential; requires significant prior investment.

## Key Physics Reminders
- Rocket equation: Δv = ve × ln(m0/mf). ve ≈ 4.4 km/s for 450 s Isp.
- Oberth effect: Burn deep in gravity well multiplies energy gain.
- Tethers transfer existing orbital/rotational energy; they must be reboosted.
